Top 10 volunteer opportunities in Airdrie

October 2026 · Shared by Volunteer Airdrie, featured free on Our Airdrie.

This month's sponsor — DealFinder

Locally owned & operated online auction house — the best deals for everything you could want or need. DealFinder.hibid.com

1

Pumpkin Fest — Bluegrass Nursery

Multiple roles at a Pumpkin Fest in support of the Alberta Children's Hospital, suitable for a variety of abilities. Ages 13+ with a guardian, 15+ solo.

October 10 13+ with a guardian Youth/family friendly
info2@bluegrassnursery.com
2

Mentors — The BeaYOUtiful Foundation

Mentors are needed for a six-week confidence-building program for female and non-binary youth ages 8 to 10. Mentors must be 18 or older.

Tuesdays Oct 20 – Nov 24 · 6–7:30 pm 18+
thebeayoutifulfoundation.com/volunteer
3

Trick or Treat at the Creek

In support of the Airdrie Food Bank, at Nose Creek Regional Park. Various positions with different shift times through the day.

October 25 · 8 am–5 pm Nose Creek Regional Park Youth/family friendly
tracy@trickortreatcreek.ca
4

50/50 Sales & Security — Airdrie Techmation Thunder

Help at Friday-night home games. Day-of training provided, ages 18+. Two to three volunteers are needed per game.

Friday home games · 6:30–10:30 pm 18+
thunderassistantgm@gmail.com
5

Call Volunteer — CNIB

Volunteer from home making 40 to 80 calls a month to participants, supporting Mobile Hubs and other CNIB programs across Alberta and the Northwest Territories. Training and support provided, ages 18+.

From home · Ongoing 18+
cnib.ca/en/support-us/volunteer
6

Committee Members — Airdrie Parades

Help plan the annual Santa Claus and Canada Day parades.

Ongoing
airdrieparades.com
7

Drivers — Drive Happiness

Help transport low-income senior residents to medical appointments in Airdrie and Calgary on weekdays.

Ongoing · Weekdays
drivehappiness.ca/volunteering
8

Snow Angels — Volunteer Airdrie

Help local low-income seniors and people with disabilities clear snow from sidewalks and driveways. All ages can volunteer alongside an adult guardian; 16+ can volunteer solo with parental consent. Brief online training and a waiver are required.

Seasonal All ages with a guardian Youth/family friendly
info@volunteerairdrie.ca
9

Volunteers — Airdrie Nordic Club

Love winter sports? This is a great role for you. Contact the club to learn more.

Seasonal
info@airdrienordicclub.ca
10

Swim Officials — Nose Creek Swimming Association

Help at swim meets throughout the season in officiating and other roles. Volunteers can start as young as 16. E-learning and screening are required.

Season 16+ Youth/family friendly
office@ncsaswim.ca

Save the date — Volunteer ConnectFest

September 20 · 1–4 pm · Airdrie Public Library. Meet local non-profits face-to-face to learn about their volunteer needs. Complimentary on-site childcare. Hosted by Volunteer Airdrie.
