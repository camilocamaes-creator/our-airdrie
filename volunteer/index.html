Top 10 volunteer opportunities in Airdrie

August / September 2026 · Shared by Volunteer Airdrie, featured free on Our Airdrie.

This month's sponsor — DealFinder

1

Family Festival — Sparrow's Table

Help at a family festival for the clients of this food-security non-profit (invitation-only attendance). Roles include setup, food prep, running yard and carnival games, supervising play areas, and handing out back-to-school supplies. First aid volunteers, hair stylists, and face painters are also needed. Ages 16+ can volunteer solo; younger helpers welcome with prior arrangement.

August 22 · 3–8 pm Jensen Park Youth/family friendly
hello@thesparrowstable.ca · 587.890.7843
2

Demolition Derby — Crossfield Elks #416

A community fundraiser where all proceeds go back to the community of Crossfield. Call or text Alan for details on how to help.

August 29 Crossfield
Call or text Alan: (403) 512-2787
3

Casino Volunteers — Rocky View Regional Handibus Society

Volunteer at this casino fundraiser supporting accessible transportation across Rocky View. Volunteers must be 18 or older.

September 4 & 5 18+
bit.ly/4eQvYtR
4

airdrieFEST — Volunteer Airdrie

Airdrie's big community festival on Main Street. Multiple shift times and roles from as early as 8 am to as late as 5:30 pm. Ages 12+ (16+ to volunteer solo). Free childcare available for volunteers with prior arrangement.

September 12 · Main Street Youth/family friendly
bit.ly/airdriefest · info@volunteerairdrie.ca
5

Artwalk — Airdrie Arts & Culture Council

A free outdoor art event at Inspire. Help the day run smoothly for artists and visitors.

September 12 · 11 am–4 pm Inspire
hello@airdrieartsculturecouncil.com
6

Peace in the Park — The Thumbs Up Foundation

Certified first-aid volunteers are needed for this music festival, which brings the therapeutic power of music to the community and raises awareness about mental health.

September 19 First aid certified
Doreen@thethumbsupfoundation.com
7

Mealtime Companions — Bethany

Help create a positive, supportive dining experience for residents by offering one-on-one assistance and friendly conversation during meals.

Ongoing
bethanyseniors.com/work-with-us/volunteer
8

Good Grief Walking Companion — Airdrie Hospice

Guide weekly grief-support walks, ensuring safety, reflection, and connection throughout the journey. Strong listening skills, comfort discussing loss, and compassion are a must. Orientation provided.

Ongoing · Tuesdays 9:45 am Genesis Place
contact@airdriehospice.ca · 403.512.0194
9

Members — Airdrie Kin Club

Join a co-ed social service club that serves the community's greatest needs through service, leadership, fellowship, and personal development.

Ongoing
airdriekinclub.com
10

Food Distribution & Childcare — The Sparrow's Table

Saturday mornings at the old United Church (216 – 1 Ave NE). Volunteers can bring their children, as childcare is provided (childcare volunteers need a vulnerable-sector check). Ongoing help is also needed for emergency donation pickups and handouts, building cleanup, and fundraising events.

Saturday mornings 216 – 1 Ave NE Youth/family friendly
hello@thesparrowstable.ca · 587.890.7843

Save the date — Volunteer ConnectFest

September 20 · 1–4 pm · Airdrie Public Library. Meet local non-profits face-to-face to learn about their volunteer needs. Complimentary on-site childcare. Hosted by Volunteer Airdrie.
